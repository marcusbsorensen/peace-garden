# The Wild Fields' ground, close to, 2 October 2026

Renders of the ground's detail (`Server/assets/js/wildground.js`, docs/WEB-GARDENS.md §*The Wild Fields' ground, close to*). There were two swards; **Marcus chose the pasture on 2 October 2026**, and it is the only one (docs/WEB-GARDENS.md §*The Wild Fields joined*).

- **before**: the ground as it was, one smooth shaded sheet.
- **pasture** (the default): grazed short, 4 to 15 cm, with earth showing between the tussocks, rank patches left long to about 25 cm, and a tenth of the tufts gone to seed.
- **meadow**: uncut, 8 to 28 cm, thick and seeding, with hardly any earth.
- **worn-and-wet**: the pasture with its two inputs given something to say (`/dev/wild?try=both`). An invented path runs across the middle and an invented pond margin lies beside it. The worn paths and the ponds are on their own branches and will give these. The black middle of the margin is where the ponds branch lays its water.
- **phone**: the opening look on a 390 × 844 phone at 3×.

Each desktop view is 1000 × 640 at 2×, reduced to 840 px. The close ones are reduced to 256 colours to stay under 500 KB.

- **wide**: the opening look over the middle of the field, about 7 m across.
- **foot**: three times closer, at the foot of the plant nearest the middle (31.84, 31.64).
- **bare**: as close as the field goes, on the barest stony ground near the middle (26.2, 34.5). One firefly drift is in it.
- **path** and **margin**: 3.2 times closer, on the invented path's edge (29.5, 31.9) and the pond's margin (30.9, 33.0).

Every view has the same 400 invented plants (`/dev/wild?plants=400`, plus `&sward=meadow` or `&try=both`). They were drawn headless on Chrome with the Mac's GPU, with motion reduced so the fireflies hold still. The before views were drawn from 24c45e5.
