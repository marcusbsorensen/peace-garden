Motion
One gesture, at three scales: a line unfolding over seconds, a tendril over minutes, a plant over weeks. Everything in the app is a version of something opening.

Each line waits for the one before it to have been read. The gap comes from the word count — 0.5s + 0.22s per word — so a longer line is given longer, and the cadence matches a reader rather than a metronome.
The durations that exist
1.2s
A line, a heading or a control arriving. Ease in and out.
1.7s
The breathing dot, repeating, while a seed is being formed.
6s
A tendril opening and closing again at the ends of a rule.
0.55s
Controls fading in on a tap, and out again when left alone.
6s

days
The growth itself, in real time, as a pure function of elapsed days.
The breath
Used on the screens that are waiting on something. It is the app's one piece of perpetual motion, and it earns that by standing in for a heartbeat.
Reduce Motion
Holds a composed state rather than freezing mid-cycle: a tendril rests half open, the waiting stops, the fades stay. The objection is to being made to wait through choreography, not to a crossfade.
Where a coil relaxes
A tendril opens by its curvature falling away, which is how a fiddlehead does it. Trimming a fixed spiral reads as a line being drawn on, and is a duller gesture for it.
