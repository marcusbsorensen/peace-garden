Peace Garden
Visual language
A plant grown from a seed drawn once for one person. Two people who meet cross their seeds and grow a plant that belongs to them both.
The idea everything follows from
A plant is a seed plus a birthday.
Everything else is derived.
No appearance is stored, sent or synced. It is why two phones agree with no server, why a garden is kilobytes, and why a saved plant holds its shape forever.
Six principles
Unfolding, not appearing
Everything arrives at the pace it is taken in — a line at a time, a plant over weeks. A screen that appears whole is a screen to be scanned.
Sized against what it will become
A seedling is small in a large space and spends weeks growing into it. Scale carries the whole of what there is to watch.
The plant holds the colour
It is the one saturated thing on screen. Every control stays quiet enough to be forgotten between taps.
Say what a thing is
State it positively. Defining something by what it is not leaves everything else standing as an open question.
Marks are monoline and symbolic
One even stroke, round free ends, one gradient along it. Governed by the Interfulgent suite, which owns the ground and the treatment.
Room, and time
Generous dark space around one small living thing. The garden keeps its own time and everything arrives.
The suite brand — ground, stroke treatment, gradient and hue allocation — is governed by suite-brand.md in the UnCubed repository. This canvas covers what Peace Garden owns.
