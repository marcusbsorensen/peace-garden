Layouts for the ten areas

Each area as it is now, beside two proposals that keep its rule and its meaning but drop the grid. Now is the workbench, captured; A and B are plans with north up, at ten plants and as a settled plot after a thousand invented arrivals. Plants are blobs coloured by habit, with a dot of their flower colour. The reasons and the costs are in RESEARCH.md beside this page.
