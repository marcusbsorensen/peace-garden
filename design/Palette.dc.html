Palette
Two families. The base is where everything lives; the highlight belongs to the mark and to the moments the app is being celebratory. The plant's own colours are derived per seed and sit outside both.
Base — the ground and the chrome
#0D0D1F  Ground
The suite ground, flat, in every appearance. Cool rather than warm: red minus blue is −18.
Ink  white
Headings, the name a person is typing, the one prominent control on a screen.
Muted  white 55%
Body copy, and every hint that explains the app to someone meeting it.
Faint  white 28%
Labels, field prompts, the growth stage under a plant, tendrils.
Hairline  white 16%
Rules, and nothing else. Used sparingly enough to stay a punctuation mark.

All three stops sit at one luminance — spread 0.000% — because a triad that drifts in value becomes a staircase in greyscale. Lightness is solved per hue against a luminance target rather than chosen: yellow is the extreme case, and eyeballing it is how the sweep goes wrong.
Where the highlight belongs
The app icon's stroke, and the rare celebratory moment. It stays off ordinary chrome: a control tinted with it competes with the plant, which is the one thing on screen allowed to be saturated. The spadix inside the icon's bract is flat #FFFFFF and takes no part in the gradient.
