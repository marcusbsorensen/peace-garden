Marks and imagery
Line, not fill. Everything drawn in this app is a stroke of even weight with round free ends, which is what lets a tendril, a rule and the app icon read as one hand.
The mark
An unwinding logarithmic spiral carrying a peace lily's spathe, with the spadix inside it. One continuous stroke; the spathe leaves on the tangent the spiral ends on, so the whole thing is one gesture.
Sketched here. The drawn mark comes from tools/icon/make_icon.py, which is the only place it may be changed.
What may be depicted
A mark may show what the app gives you. Showing what the app is about is the case the suite forbids, in the way that a flag is a flag. The spiral is safe either way: it depicts unfolding over time, which is the medium rather than the subject.
Holds
Monoline strokes. Round free ends, square joins where a stroke meets another. One gradient along the stroke, bottom-left to top-right. Flat ground.
Stays out
Gradients on the ground, shadows, bevels, a drawn squircle, arbitrary filled shapes, and any stroke whose weight varies without the suite's terms being met.
Imagery in the app
The plant
Rendered geometry, derived from the seed. The one saturated thing on screen, and the only photograph-like object the app has.
Drawn line
Tendrils, rules, the mark. Hairline or faint, one weight, always in service of something rather than as ornament on its own.
Light
A pool of it, tinted to the plant's own palette, giving the plant somewhere to stand. It is the only background the app has.
Photographs, stock illustration and icon sets all stay out: the app draws one living thing and everything else is a line.
