# `privacy9`, paths that visitors wear, 2 October 2026

The ninth privacy paragraph, Marcus's English of 2 October, in 41 languages
(every catalogue but `kl`, which awaits a speaker). Written from
`commission.py --privacy <code>` and its `WEAR` claims, in one pass, using each
language's `wildTitle` and the verbs its `privacy8` already uses for what the
Wild Fields keep. No numeral is a quantity here; *a few crossings* stays a few.
Half a metre is written as each language says it. `check.py`: 42 clean, 41 with
the paths paragraph. Nobody who speaks any of these has read it yet.

One line per language: what I was unsure of.

- **da** — *firkanter af jorden* for the squares (*felter* read like a board game); *trampestier* for the paths, which is exactly where grass has been walked; *folk* for people. *Udsyn* for view.
- **ar** — Masculine address, as `privacy8`; *عدد* for the number (a count) where `privacy8` uses *رقم* for the meeting's; no numerals.
- **be** — *лік* for the number and *падлік* for the count; *агляд* for the view is my least certain word.
- **bg** — *изглед* for the view; *минавания* for crossings is plain but slightly clumsy.
- **ca** — *trepitjat* for worn, and *camins* for paths; *corriols* was the other choice.
- **cs** — *pěšinky* for paths; *procházíš* may lean towards strolling, which the claims allow only here.
- **cy** — *sathredig* for worn (trodden); *croesiadau* for crossings.
- **de** — *Quadrate Boden* and *Trampelpfade*; *wie ausgetreten es ist* for worn.
- **el** — *οπτικό πεδίο* for the view; Greek *·* for the semicolon.
- **es** — *pisado* for worn and *pasadas* for crossings.
- **et** — *maaruudud* for squares of ground; *ületamine* for crossing, avoiding *ülekäik* (a pedestrian crossing).
- **eu** — Written with *zu*, as the catalogue's `privacy*` are; *bidezidor* for path. Least sure of the sentence order in the first sentence.
- **fi** — *ruutu* for square may read slightly grid-like; *tallattu* for worn.
- **fr** — *foulé* for worn; the *quelle que soit… quel que soit* sentence is long.
- **ga** — Least sure of all: *satailte* for worn, *cosáin* for paths, and the idiom in *fad is a choinníonn daoine orthu á shiúl*. Wants a speaker.
- **gl** — *pisado*, *camiños*; mirrors the Spanish closely, which may be too close.
- **he** — Masculine address, as `privacy8`; *כמה דרכו עליו* for how worn it is.
- **hr** — *utaban* for worn; *na ovoj stranici… stranica* repeats, as `privacy5` already calls the site *stranica*.
- **hu** — *földnégyzet* for square of ground; *kitaposott* for worn.
- **is** — *reitir* for squares, a gardener's word; *troðinn* for worn.
- **it** — *calpestato* and *sentieri*; no doubts beyond that.
- **ja** — Half a metre written as *50センチ四方* and half a minute as *30秒ほど*, which is how Japanese says both; *区画* for squares and *小道* for paths.
- **ko** — Same choices as Japanese: *50센티미터*, *30초쯤*; *구획* for squares may read a little formal.
- **lt** — Impersonal gerunds (*tempiant*, *spaudžiant*) to avoid a gendered participle; *išmindžiotas* for worn.
- **lv** — *nomīdīts* for worn; *pusmetru platu* for half a metre across.
- **mk** — *изгазен* for worn; *страница* for both site and page, as `privacy5` does.
- **mt** — *mirfus* for worn and *mogħdijiet* for paths; Maltese wants a speaker.
- **nb** — *firkanter av bakken*; *stier* rather than *tråkk*, which was tempting.
- **nl** — *platgelopen* for worn; *mensen* for people.
- **pl** — *wydeptany* and *ścieżki*; *strona* for both site and page.
- **pt** — European, with *tu*; *trilhos* for paths, *pisado* for worn.
- **ro** — *bătătorit* for worn and *poteci* for paths.
- **ru** — *ходишь* rather than *гуляешь*, so walk is not a stroll; *вытоптан* for worn.
- **sk** — *chodníčky* for paths; *na tomto webe* to avoid repeating *stránka*.
- **sl** — *shojen* for worn; *steze* for paths.
- **sq** — *i shkelur* for worn and *shtigje* for paths; agreement of *të renditur* with *listë* worth a look.
- **sr** — Cyrillic, as the catalogue; *угажен* for worn, *сортиран* for sorted.
- **sv** — *rutor av marken* may read grid-like; *nött* for worn; *folk* for people.
- **tr** — *çiğnendiğini* for worn and *patika* for path; the first sentence is long.
- **uk** — *півметра завширшки*; *витоптаний* for worn.
- **zh** — Simplified, as the catalogue; *半米见方* and *小路*; *被踩过多少* for how worn.
